# Franken-E58 PCB decision log

One row per design choice. Date in ISO form. State: Decided, Default (open), Proposed, Open, Rejected. Decisions carried in from the Phase 2 blocks (`docs/Phase2_blueprint_blocks.md`, Block 1) are listed first; new decisions are appended below them as they are made.

| Date | ID | Decision | Reason | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-01 | Single PCB with the ESP32-S3 module soldered on. No dev board, no shield | A 44-pin dev board is too tall and heavy for a micro-quad cavity; mass and height are the main constraints | Decided |
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-02 | Module: ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 by default | Matches the module family validated in Phase 1 (flash and PSRAM modes, `sdkconfig`); any other module needs the §4.9 options re-verified | Default, open (close in P2-2, O3) |
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-03 | USB-C receptacle with D+ and D− on GPIO 20 and 19 (native USB Serial/JTAG) as the programming, logging and debug port; BOOT and EN buttons and UART0 pads as fallback | Same native port as Phase 1; no USB-UART bridge needed | Decided in principle (height and mass checked in P2-1, power path in P2-2) |
| 2026-11-12 | D-P2-03 | Fit check: **go**. Receptacle on the aircraft-left long edge, tail half (y 3 to 13), flush with the board edge, reached through a 7 x 13 mm slot cut in the frame side wall (1 to 1.5 mm thick, solid, free of latches). Height clears the canopy by over 20 mm. Alternative (vertical receptacle through a canopy hole) rejected by the user | User prefers the side cut: easier and tidier than a top hole; the frame wall there is thin and empty | Decided (exact y and part in P2-3) |
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-04 | IMU at the geometric centre and centre of gravity, away from the antenna, LDO, module and motor currents, over solid ground; whole board soft-mounted | Least noise (R1) | Decided (slot or cut-out option reserved) |
| 2026-11-12 | D-P2-04 | IMU at the geometric centre of the board outline (11.5, 23.25). No centre-of-gravity measurement: the aircraft cannot be assembled in P2-1 | Default from the package; the OEM board sits symmetrically in the frame so the geometric centre is the best available estimate | Decided (re-check against a balance measurement when the aircraft is assembled) |
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-05 | Camera: mechanical only in Rev A (mount and cable clearance kept, no electrical connection) | The video and Wi-Fi chip is probably on the OEM board, and a DVP camera needs about 12 GPIOs | Superseded 2026-11-12 (below) |
| 2026-11-12 | D-P2-05 | **Camera strictly mechanical in Rev A.** The OEM camera adapter PCB (Beken BK7231UQN40 Wi-Fi/BT SoC, video on board), both 20-pin FPC cameras and their mounts stay in the camera compartment under the board's nose half, and the new board keeps that compartment clear below it (no bottom-side parts on the nose half other than the battery header). **No electrical connection** to the camera system: no connector, no footprint, no 0 Ω hook. The camera feed is not used in any way; any future use needs a board redesign | P2-1 group F inspection: the OEM camera system reaches the flight board only by a 3-wire lead (V+, GND, one-way serial into RX), so there is no video interface to carry. Streaming video over the same Wi-Fi link as the control traffic would disturb control, so it is not wanted | **Decided** |
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-06 | ToF: breakout on a connector in Rev A, chip on board later | The VL53L1X is a small optical LGA that needs a cover window | Proposed (confirm in P2-2) |
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-07 | Assembly method: hand-solder or fab assembly | Decides package choices (LGA, QFN, passive size) | Open (default fab assembly of SMD parts; close in P2-2) |
| 2026-11-12 | D-P2-07 | Preference recorded: the user hand-solders all components | Stated by the user in P2-1. Drives part selection in P2-2 towards hand-solderable packages (no LGA, QFN only with exposed leads or a hot-air plan, passives 0603 or larger) | Preference (closed in P2-2) |
| 2026-11-11 | D-P2-08 | Power path between USB 5 V and the 1S battery | Phase 3 needs USB logging while the motors run from the battery; VBUS must never back-feed the battery or reach the motor rail | Open (close in P2-2; default option B, source-select jumper) |
